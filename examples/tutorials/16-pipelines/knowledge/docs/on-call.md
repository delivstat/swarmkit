# On-call handbook

## Rota

The platform team runs a weekly rota, Monday 09:00 to Monday 09:00 IST. The primary carries the
pager; the secondary is paged only when the primary does not acknowledge within 15 minutes.

## Severity

| Level | Meaning | Response |
|---|---|---|
| SEV1 | Customer-facing outage | Acknowledge within 5 minutes, incident channel opened, status page updated |
| SEV2 | Degraded service, workaround exists | Acknowledge within 30 minutes |
| SEV3 | No customer impact | Next working day |

## Handover

The outgoing primary writes a handover note in the on-call channel before 09:00 Monday: open
incidents, noisy alerts silenced, anything due to expire.

## Compensation

On-call weeks are compensated at a flat ₹8,000 plus time off in lieu for any SEV1 worked outside
office hours.
