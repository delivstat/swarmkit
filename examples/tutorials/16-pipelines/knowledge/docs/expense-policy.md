# Expense policy

Version 3, effective 1 March 2026.

## Meals

- Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to **₹2,500 per day** (or the local equivalent).
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.
- Team meals need a manager's approval when the total exceeds ₹10,000.

## Travel

- Book flights through the travel desk. Economy for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is
  allowed on longer flights.
- Hotels are capped at ₹9,000 per night in metros and ₹6,000 elsewhere.
- Ground transport: taxis are fine; keep the receipt.

## Submitting

Submit within 30 days of the expense through the finance portal. Receipts are required for
anything over ₹500. Reimbursement lands in the next payroll run after approval.
